# Multi-Touch Marketing Attribution

## Introduction

This project was completed as part of my learning and practical experience in marketing analytics and data analysis.

The idea behind this project was to understand how customers interact with different marketing channels before making a conversion. Instead of assuming that only the first or last interaction matters, I explored multiple attribution models to see how channel performance changes under different approaches.

Through this project, I worked with customer journey data, analyzed marketing performance, calculated key business metrics, and generated insights that could help improve marketing decisions.

---

## What I Worked On

During this project, I:

* Loaded and cleaned multiple datasets
* Performed exploratory data analysis (EDA)
* Analyzed marketing spend across channels
* Studied customer journey patterns
* Implemented different attribution models
* Calculated important marketing KPIs
* Compared channel performance
* Generated business recommendations based on the findings

---

## Attribution Models Explored

I implemented and compared the following attribution models:

### First Touch Attribution

Credits the first interaction that brought the customer into the journey.

### Last Touch Attribution

Credits the final interaction before conversion.

### Linear Attribution

Distributes credit equally across all touchpoints.

### Time Decay Attribution

Assigns more credit to interactions that happen closer to the conversion event.

## What I Learned

One of the biggest takeaways from this project was that marketing performance can look very different depending on the attribution model being used.

A channel that appears highly successful under one model may receive much less credit under another. This showed me why businesses need to look beyond simple last-click attribution when making marketing decisions.

I also gained hands-on experience working with real-world style datasets, data cleaning, visualization, and translating analytical findings into business recommendations.
